# Evaluation Report: energy-use model

**Task:** predict annual purchased energy intensity (electricity + gas), kWh per m2 per year.
**Split:** by property, seed 42. Train 1386, validation 297, test 297. No property appears in more than one split.
**Features (available at prediction time):** property_type, region, energy_rating, heating, insulation, glazing, floor_area, age, solar, smart_controls. Property value, sale prices and energy readings are not used as inputs.
**Missing data:** 20 homes dropped for missing energy data (missing months are never treated as zero).
**Model:** HistGradientBoostingRegressor (scikit-learn). Hyperparameters chosen on validation only: {'learning_rate': 0.05, 'max_iter': 200, 'max_depth': 3}.
**Baseline:** mean kWh/m2 by property type, fitted on train only.

## Validation results
| Model | MAE (kWh/m2) | RMSE (kWh/m2) | R2 | MAE (kWh/year) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 43.3 | 56.1 | -0.007 | 4,314 |
| Model | 26.1 | 34.6 | 0.617 | 2,569 |

## Limitations
- The dataset is synthetic. Energy values were generated by documented rules (poorer insulation, older homes, single glazing, gas boilers and lower ratings use more energy per m2, with seasonal patterns and noise). The model learning those rules does not prove it would predict real homes accurately.
- Metrics are only comparable with the baseline on this same dataset.
- The model predicts energy intensity. Retrofit savings, carbon and value are calculated by the rule-based engine, not by this model.
- Predictions are estimates, not guarantees.
